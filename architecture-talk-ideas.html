Architecture Run Sheets

Five ways to spend the thirty minutes before your kata workshop. Each one is a full minute-by-minute run sheet with attributed war stories, audience cues and a hand-off into the hands-on part. Steal across tabs freely; the Mix & Match tab shows where each one is strongest.

The five beats every talk must hit

  1. ACompanies. How architecture actually happens in different kinds of organisations.
  2. BRoles. What it looks like from dev, senior, PO, SM and management chairs.
  3. CHard. Why it is genuinely difficult, not just under-practised.
  4. DEasier. Concrete habits that make it lighter and more natural.
  5. EEveryone. Why juniors and non-devs belong in the room, and how to get them talking.
Stories are summarised from public sources (books, published talks, incident reports, blog posts) and attributed on each card so you can verify wording before you quote it on stage. Money figures are the commonly reported ones.
